1. Transfer Learning Power

Training deep neural networks on 15,000 images from scratch takes massive time and risks severe overfitting. We leverage EfficientNetB0 pre-trained on 1.4 million ImageNet photos, which already recognizes edges, gradients, and leaf textures.

  • Compound scaling across width, depth, and resolution
  • Only 4.8M parameters (5x smaller than ResNet50)
  • Quantizable to < 5MB via TensorFlow Lite
🔄

2. Two-Stage Fine-Tuning

To avoid catastrophic forgetting of ImageNet features, training proceeds in two calibrated phases:

  • Stage 1 (Feature Extraction): Base model frozen. Only the new classification head trains at LR = 0.001.
  • Stage 2 (Fine-Tuning): Top 50% of base model unfrozen at LR = 0.0001 (10x smaller) to adapt deep filters for tomato leaf pathology.
  • Automatic class weight balancing for rare classes.
🛡️

3. Regularization & Generalization

Plant images collected in varied weather need robust safeguards against memorization:

  • Dropout (0.5 and 0.3): Zeroes out random units to force redundant feature learning.
  • Batch Normalization: Normalizes activations and stabilizes gradient flow.
  • Early Stopping: Automatically halts training when validation loss stops improving.

⚡ 60-Second Elevator Pitch

"In agriculture, plant diseases destroy over 20% of annual crop yields, and manual lab diagnosis is too slow to stop outbreaks. My project is an end-to-end Deep Learning Tomato Leaf Disease Diagnosis System powered by Transfer Learning with EfficientNetB0. Trained on 15,139 real leaf images across 10 disease categories, it achieves over 98% accuracy. Instead of just outputting a label, it functions as a digital agronomist: ranking top candidates, assessing severity, and recommending organic bio-fungicides and chemical treatment plans. It also exports lightweight TFLite models for deployment on mobile edge devices for farmers in the field."
